HIS LIFE

A Life Dedicated to India

Dr. Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam was an Indian aerospace scientist and the 11th President of India. He was born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu. His journey from a modest childhood to becoming one of India's most respected scientists and leaders inspired millions of people.

Kalam played an important role in India's space and defence programmes. He worked with the Indian Space Research Organisation and the Defence Research and Development Organisation. His contribution to India's missile development programmes earned him the popular title "Missile Man of India".

In 2002, Kalam became the 11th President of India. During his presidency, he connected strongly with students and young people. He encouraged them to dream big, develop knowledge and contribute to the development of the nation.

Kalam received several honours during his lifetime, including the Bharat Ratna, India's highest civilian award. He continued teaching, writing and interacting with students after his presidency. He passed away on 27 July 2015 while delivering a lecture at IIM Shillong.

HIS LEGACY

Inspiring Generations

Dr. Kalam's life continues to inspire students, scientists and young leaders across India. His focus on education, scientific thinking and national development made him a respected figure far beyond his role as a scientist and President.

His books, speeches and interactions with students continue to encourage young people to pursue knowledge, set goals and work towards building a better future.
